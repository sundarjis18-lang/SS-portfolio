About the Book

Wings of Fire is an inspiring autobiography of Dr. A.P.J. Abdul Kalam. It describes his journey from a humble childhood in Rameswaram to becoming a renowned scientist and the President of India. The book highlights his struggles, achievements, leadership, and dedication to serving the nation.

Tenacity & Engineering

Overcoming initial launch failures to pioneer ISRO's satellite launch systems and strategic defense architectures.

Leadership & Accountability

Leading multidisciplinary scientific teams with absolute trust, humility, and owning failure while celebrating the team's success.

Humility & National Service

Remaining rooted in modesty, continuous learning, and selfless commitment to building a self-reliant nation.
